About

Research with an outward trajectory

My work sits at the intersection of international business, global trade and supply chain resilience. I am particularly interested in how firms respond to disruption, policy change and uncertainty, and how academic research can be translated into useful insight for organisations, decision-makers and wider public audiences.

Alongside research and publication, my work increasingly connects with applied projects, consultancy, commercialisation activity, public commentary and academic leadership.

Teaching & Academic Leadership

Research-informed education

My teaching and programme leadership draw directly on international business research and contemporary global challenges. I am particularly interested in strengthening the theoretical core of business education while keeping learning connected to live organisational problems and professional practice.

My academic leadership has included curriculum development, student project pathways, external engagement and initiatives designed to create sustained value for students and programmes.
