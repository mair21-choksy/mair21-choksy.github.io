Research

A supplier-centred view of global value chains

My work contributes to international business and global value chain research by shifting attention towards upstream suppliers, particularly those in developing economies. Rather than viewing global production primarily through the decisions of lead firms, I examine how suppliers experience and respond to disruption, power asymmetries, sustainability pressures and changing global conditions.

Across this research, I am interested in how suppliers build resilience and pursue different forms of upgrading, including economic, social and environmental upgrading. This supplier-centred perspective provides the connecting thread across my research portfolio.

01

Global Value Chains

How suppliers are positioned, governed and connected within cross-border production systems, and how these positions shape their strategic choices and outcomes.

02

Resilience & Disruption

How upstream suppliers anticipate, absorb and adapt to shocks and uncertainty across global production networks.

03

Sustainability & Upgrading

How suppliers pursue economic, social and environmental upgrading while responding to sustainability expectations and resource constraints.

04

Trade Policy & International Business

How tariffs, policy change and wider global shifts affect firms, supply relationships and strategic responses across borders.
